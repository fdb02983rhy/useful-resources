# Agentic engineering

Resources about designing, supervising, and validating software-engineering work
performed with AI agents.

- [L8 Principal's Agentic Engineering Workflow](../resources/2026/l8-principals-agentic-engineering-workflow.md)
  — a terminal-centered workflow for memory, skills, planning, validation,
  long-running tasks, worktrees, and multi-agent orchestration.
- [git push no-mistakes](../resources/2026/no-mistakes.md) — an agent-driven
  validation gate using isolated worktrees and human escalation.
- [cmux](../resources/2026/cmux.md) — a terminal workspace for observing and
  automating multiple coding-agent sessions.
- [Grill Me](../resources/2026/grill-me.md) — a stateless planning interview that
  resolves a dependency-ordered decision tree.
- [Grill with Docs](../resources/2026/grill-with-docs.md) — a planning interview
  that also maintains repository vocabulary and selected ADRs.
- [Ponytail](../resources/2026/ponytail.md) — a portable anti-overengineering
  policy and review toolkit for coding agents.
